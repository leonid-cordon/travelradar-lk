Planning brief

Decide whether this is a coast trip, a city trip or a mix

Turkey is flexible, but that flexibility can blur the plan. Antalya is easy for resorts, Istanbul is dense and cultural, Bodrum feels more polished, and Cappadocia is a separate highlight.

Resort modeChoose Antalya or nearby resorts for beaches, all-inclusive hotels and simple logistics.
City modeChoose Istanbul when food, history, neighborhoods and walking matter more than beach time.
Premium coastLook at Bodrum for a more stylish Aegean mood and boutique hotels.
Planning orderMatch the region to the trip style, then compare flights, hotel area, insurance and eSIM.
Planning step

Flights to Turkey

Turkey is well connected year-round. Istanbul is the strongest hub, while Antalya, Izmir, Dalaman and Bodrum can make beach trips much easier.

City or coast

Match airport to trip

Use Istanbul for city breaks and coastal airports when the resort is the goal.

Season

Watch the season

Summer prices rise on the coast; spring and autumn often feel better value.

Transfers

Check transfers

Some resorts are 20 minutes from the airport; others are closer to two hours.

Compare airport options before you commit to a resort region.

Travel calmly

Insurance

Insurance is useful for Turkey even on a simple trip, and more important if you plan scooters, boat days, hiking, skiing or long intercity routes.

Baseline

Choose at least $30,000 medical coverage before departure.

Activities

Add coverage for active plans: scooters, diving, hiking or winter sports.

Documents

Keep policy details offline with your hotel address and passport copy.

Buy the policy online before departure and check activity exclusions.

Stay connected

eSIM

eSIM is especially useful in Turkey: maps, ferries, ride apps, restaurant bookings and hotel chats all depend on data.

Arrival

Install before the flight so airport transfers and maps work immediately.

City use

Istanbul can burn data quickly through maps and transport apps.

Backup

Keep your main SIM active for banking codes, eSIM for mobile data.

Set up internet before departure and skip roaming surprises.

Experiences

Tours

Turkey day trips can be excellent, but distances are real. Pick experiences that fit your base instead of trying to cover the whole country from one hotel.

Cappadocia

Balloons, valleys and cave hotels; best as a dedicated stop.

Pamukkale

White terraces and ancient Hierapolis, usually a full-day trip.

Istanbul

Mosques, palaces, bazaars and Bosphorus routes.

Yacht trips

Coastal bays, swim stops and a slower Aegean day.

Check travel time and pickup point before choosing the prettiest tour photo.

Practical notes

Good to know

Turkey is easy to enjoy when you plan around distances, seasons and neighborhood choice.

Best months

May-June and September-October are excellent for comfort and value.

Payments

Cards are common, but cash helps in markets, taxis and smaller places.

Neighborhoods

In Istanbul, area choice affects every day; stay close to what you want to do.

Transport

Use official transfers or reliable apps when arriving late.
