Travel Radar LK — an independent travel information project

✈ Travel Radar LK is an independent travel information project with practical recommendations on choosing accommodation and planning trips.

The project was created as a convenient guide for those planning a trip who want to quickly figure out:

  • where to stay in a city
  • which neighborhoods suit different needs and budgets
  • which hotels genuinely deliver on their price
  • how much a vacation actually costs

The main focus is on practical information: location, convenience, value for money, and the real pros and cons of each option. No surface-level reviews or unnecessary fluff.

All content is based on the author's personal experience as well as analysis and comparison of accommodation options. This allows for more objective and useful information to support decision-making.

Special attention is paid to visual details. A professional background in photo and video helps notice nuances that often go unmentioned in standard reviews.

About the Author

The project is run by an author based in Lviv who regularly travels across Europe and popular tourist destinations including Turkey, the Dominican Republic, and other countries.

The site may include affiliate links to booking services. This does not affect the cost for the user, but helps support and sustain the project while keeping it independent.

If you're planning a trip — here you'll find structured, clear, and genuinely useful information without the noise.
