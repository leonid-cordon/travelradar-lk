Our Mission

Choosing the right neighborhood matters more than choosing any single hotel.

Our Analytical Approach

Travel Radar LK is built to help travelers make sharper, better-informed trip decisions: where to stay, what transportation actually costs, and how to see past resort marketing claims before booking.

We do not publish superficial, overly enthusiastic reviews. Instead, we focus on clear, honest, thoroughly researched breakdowns built around how destinations work on the ground.

Micro-Area Breakdown

We break large resort zones — such as Cancun's Hotel Zone or Tulum — into distinct micro-neighborhoods, detailing the real trade-offs of each beach and stretch.

Real Transfer & Hidden Costs

We map airport transfers, ferry links, and distances from real infrastructure. A cheaper room far from everything often ends up costing significantly more.

Editorial Process

How Every Guide Is Built

Every destination breakdown and hotel checklist moves through four structured research stages before publication.

  1. 01

    Guest Review Patterns

    Analyzing thousands of recent traveler reviews to spot recurring complaints and strengths beyond surface star ratings.

  2. 02

    Maps & Logistics

    Checking satellite geography, beach access, sargassum exposure, airport drive times, and local transit routes.

  3. 03

    True-Cost Audit

    Factoring in transfers, resort fees, local taxes, and realistic daily spending so there are no arrival surprises.

  4. 04

    Regional & Editorial Review

    Cross-checking local context with regional specialists followed by final Editor-in-Chief sign-off.

Editorial Policy

Our Standards & Independence

How we keep every recommendation objective, transparent, and free from paid hotel placements.

Facts Over Opinions

We prioritize geolocation data, real transfer costs, and recurring review patterns over isolated personal impressions.

Complete Independence

We do not accept payment for hotel reviews and we never sell placement in our rankings. Every recommendation comes solely from our own analysis.

Transparent Monetization

Some articles include affiliate links that may earn a small commission at zero extra cost to you — helping keep our research independent.
