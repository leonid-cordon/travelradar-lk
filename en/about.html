About

Travel Radar LK — an independent Mexico-first travel information project

Travel Radar LK is an independent travel information project built around practical travel decisions: where to stay, how to compare areas, and what to check before booking.

The project was created as a practical guide for travelers who want to quickly figure out:

  • where to stay in a city
  • which neighborhoods suit different needs and budgets
  • which hotels genuinely deliver on their price
  • how much a vacation actually costs

The main focus is on practical information: location, convenience, value for money, and the real pros and cons of each option. No surface-level reviews, generic inspiration, or unnecessary fluff.

The editorial focus now centers on Mexico and Caribbean trip planning, especially Cancun, Tulum, Playa del Carmen, and the Riviera Maya. Selected guides to Egypt and Turkey remain on the site as supporting coverage.

The content combines structured research with travel experience, accommodation analysis, and close attention to visual details. A professional background in photo and video helps notice nuances that often go unmentioned in standard reviews.

About the Author

The project is run by an author based in Lviv, Ukraine, with extensive travel experience in Turkey and the Dominican Republic and a strong long-term interest in Mexico and Caribbean travel.

Rather than relying only on broad destination summaries, the project combines personal travel background with careful research into hotel areas, transport choices, pricing, neighborhood trade-offs, and the booking decisions that shape the whole trip.

The site may include affiliate links to booking services. This does not affect the cost for the user, but helps support and sustain the project while keeping it independent.

If you're planning a trip — here you'll find structured, clear, and genuinely useful information without the noise.
