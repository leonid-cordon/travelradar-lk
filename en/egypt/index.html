Hotels

👉 Not sure which to pick: Hurghada — easier and more affordable, Sharm — more beautiful sea, Marsa Alam — quieter and less touristy

Choosing a hotel in Egypt depends on your trip style. In short:

Hurghada

Sandy beaches, easy sea entry — best choice for a first trip and family holidays

Sharm El Sheikh

Coral reefs, diving and wind-protected bays — better for active holidays and stunning sea views

Marsa Alam

Fewer tourists, nature and the best reefs — for a peaceful stay and snorkeling

Insurance

Medical insurance for Egypt is not just a formality: private clinics and hospitalization are expensive. We recommend coverage from $35,000 and including active recreation (diving/snorkeling) if you plan sea activities and excursions. Getting it online is faster and usually more cost-effective than buying "on the spot."

eSIM

eSIM is the most convenient way to get internet even before departure: you land — and you're immediately online. It's often cheaper than roaming and saves you from searching for a local SIM at the airport. For short trips — get a small package; if you travel frequently — an "all countries" service is more convenient.

Good to Know

Practical tips for a comfortable vacation in Egypt:

🗓️

When to Go

Best time: March-May and September-November. Summer is very hot.
💵

Money

Currency is Egyptian pound. Dollars and euros are accepted everywhere.

👕

Clothing

Light clothing, hat, modest clothing for excursions.

🛡️

Safety

Resorts are safe. Don't drink tap water.
💰

Tipping

Tips are customary everywhere: $1-2 per service.

📱

Internet (eSIM)

Connect eSIM in advance to have internet immediately after landing without roaming.
