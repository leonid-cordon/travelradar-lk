Planning brief

Pick the resort by sea, not by the lowest hotel price

Egypt can be brilliant or disappointing depending on the bay, beach entry and hotel standards. The right resort area does more for the trip than one extra star in the listing.

HurghadaThe easiest first choice: sandy beaches, family hotels, simpler prices and many direct flights.
Sharm El SheikhBest when the sea is the priority: reefs, protected bays and more dramatic snorkeling.
Marsa AlamQuieter, wilder and reef-focused. Great for nature, less ideal if you want nightlife.

Insurance

Travel insurance is more than a formality in Egypt. Private clinics, hospitalization and water activities can be costly, so choose proper medical coverage and add active recreation if you plan snorkeling, diving or boat trips.

Coverage

Use $35,000 as a sensible baseline; higher limits are calmer for families.

Water activities

Add diving/snorkeling coverage when the sea is part of the plan.

Assistance

Practical notes

Good to know

Egypt is easiest when you plan around heat, sea conditions, tipping and hotel standards.

Best months

March-May and September-November are comfortable; summer can be extremely hot.

Money

Keep small dollars or local cash for tips and small services.

Health

Do not drink tap water and be careful with ice outside reliable hotels.

Clothing

Pack light clothes, sun protection and modest layers for city or temple excursions.
