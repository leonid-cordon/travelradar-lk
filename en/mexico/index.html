Planning brief

Start with the area, then book the trip around it

Mexico rewards a clear plan. Cancun is easiest for a classic beach holiday, Downtown stretches the budget, Tulum feels more boutique, and the Riviera Maya works best when you want resorts plus day trips.

First-timersChoose Cancun Hotel Zone if you want beachfront hotels, simple transfers and a smooth first trip.
Value seekersLook at Cancun Downtown or Playa del Carmen when price, restaurants and excursions matter more than a private beach.
Style-focused tripsPick Tulum only if the boutique/jungle mood is worth the higher prices and extra transport time.
Planning orderCompare flights, choose the area on the hotel map, then add insurance, eSIM and day trips.

Travel calmly

Travel insurance

Medical care and private clinics can be expensive. For Mexico, a policy with at least $50,000 coverage is the calm baseline, especially if you plan cenotes, scooters, boats or long transfers.

Medical baseline

Choose solid coverage before departure; buying after an incident will not help.

Active plans

Add activity coverage for snorkeling, diving, scooters and adventure tours.

Documents

Practical notes

Good to know

A few decisions make Mexico much easier: season, payment setup, transport and realistic distances.

Season

December-April is most comfortable and expensive; late summer and autumn can bring heat, rain and storms.

Money

Cards work widely in resorts, but keep cash for tips, buses, markets and small beach spots.

Transfers

Book airport transfer if you arrive late or stay outside Cancun.

Hotel checks

Read recent reviews for beach condition, seaweed, construction and noise.
