Punta Cana Airport Guide: eTicket, Transfers and Arrival Scams

PUJ looks relaxed under its palm roofs. The confusing part begins after customs, when five helpful voices claim to know where you should go.

Warm air reaches the cabin door, the terminal opens beneath palm-thatch roofs, and a winter coat becomes an immediate mistake. The architecture says vacation. The logistics still belong to an international airport processing several resort flights at once.

The hard part is knowing which instruction deserves attention. The eTicket is mandatory but free. The tourist-card charge is normally inside the airfare. Authorized taxis, booked transfers and app rides are real; people offering to rescue you from an existing plan are selling a different one.

This guide gets you from preflight paperwork to the correct resort vehicle without a fake form fee or curbside negotiation. For the wider destination, read our Punta Cana guide for first-time visitors.

Quick Answer: The Calm Way Through PUJ

Complete the free eTicket on the official Dominican government website before airline check-in, save the QR code offline, and decide your ground transportation before landing. After customs, follow the name and meeting instructions in your booking instead of following the most persuasive person in the hall.

The one rule: no legitimate Dominican eTicket fee appears at the end of the official form. If a site asks for $30, $50 or a service charge, close it and check the domain.

The Dominican eTicket: Free, Mandatory and Easy to Fake Online

The eTicket combines information used by Dominican migration and customs for entry and exit. It is not a visa, not the $10 tourist card and not a paid airport fast-track. The Dirección General de Migración states that every passenger entering or leaving the country must be covered and that the online service is free.

Use the government domain

Start at eticket.migracion.gob.do. The ending matters: `gob.do` is the Dominican government domain. Sponsored search results with similar names may be private form-filling services.

Have the real trip details ready

  • passport number and full name;
  • email, airline and flight number;
  • the exact resort or accommodation address;
  • customs information when applicable.

Keep the code, not just an open browser tab

Save the QR image and application code where they work without airport Wi-Fi. The current system can email the result, save an incomplete form and create a departure eTicket from an earlier submission.

The smartest timing is after the airline, flight number and hotel are settled but before check-in opens. Dominican rules require airlines to verify that passengers have completed the form, which explains why a gate agent in the United States or Canada may ask for the QR before anyone in Punta Cana does.

One charge does belong to the trip: the $10 Dominican tourist card. For travelers arriving by air, both the airport and the U.S. State Department say it is included in airline charges. Check with the carrier if your itinerary is unusual, but do not pay a second person at PUJ simply because they call the eTicket a tourist card.

Prepare the Arrival Before You Board

"Airport pickup included" is not a meeting plan. Save the operator's name, phone number, terminal instructions and the exact sign or logo. Airport Wi-Fi exists, but a failed login is a poor reason to improvise at the curb.

Send the flight number, not just a landing time, so the driver can track a delay. For a shared tour-operator bus, confirm whether its desk is at the end of customs or outside; those are different meeting points.

Check the luggage-to-vehicle math too. Four adults plus four checked bags may require a van, and a cheap transfer needing a second car is no bargain. Our Punta Cana area guide explains why Cap Cana and Uvero Alto also create different airport days.

Useful backup: write the resort name and address separately from the booking email. Several properties share similar brand names, and saying only "the Riu" or "the Bahia" is not a destination.

PUJ Arrival, Step by Step

Line length changes with flight banks, staffing and baggage. The order does not: keep moving through the same five handoffs.

Plane to terminal
Follow arrivals, not the first open doorway

Some flights use stairs or a bus. Keep passport and QR accessible and expect the heat.

Immigration
Officer line or eligible automated gate

PUJ lists eGates for US and Canadian travelers age 21 and older. Availability can change, so follow staff.

Baggage claim
Collect every bag before customs

Check the belt screen, inspect bags before leaving and keep claim tags.

Customs
Use the lane and instructions assigned

The eTicket carries customs data, but officers may still direct passengers or bags for inspection.

Ground transport
Match the operator, sign and meeting point

Tour-operator information begins at the end of customs. Match it to your saved instructions.

After customs, the atmosphere turns commercial. That does not make PUJ unsafe; it means several businesses want your attention before you reach the curb.

The Arrivals-Hall Gauntlet: Helpful Is Not the Same as Yours

Travelers call the people between customs and the curb "the sharks," but the useful distinction is your contracted representative versus everyone else. A legitimate airport business can still have nothing to do with your booking.

Mistake 01

Showing your confirmation to the first person who asks. It gives a salesperson enough information to sound connected. Ask them to state your name or reference first.

Mistake 02

Believing your driver was canceled. Call the number in the original confirmation before letting another desk solve an unverified problem.

Mistake 03

Letting luggage create an unspoken transaction. A porter normally expects a tip. Say no clearly if you want to keep the bag.

Mistake 04

Accepting an off-app rideshare offer. Request in the app, then verify the driver, plate and vehicle.

Do this instead: keep walking at a normal pace, say "No, thank you," and look for the exact logo or meeting point in your confirmation. You do not owe anyone your hotel, room number, phone number or vacation plans.

A current operator advertised a $35 starting private-vehicle quote from PUJ to Bávaro for up to six passengers when checked in August 2026. For four, that is $8.75 each. It is not an airport tariff, but it proves the rule: compare the whole vehicle, luggage and waiting terms against a per-person shuttle.

Your Resort Name Does Not Tell You the Drive Time

Booking sites use "Punta Cana" for a long resort coast, not one compact hotel zone. Dominican Republic Tourism gives a clean official baseline from PUJ:

  • Punta Cana and Cap Cana: about 15 minutes;
  • Bávaro, El Cortecito and Arena Gorda: about 30 minutes;
  • Macao and Uvero Alto: about 45 minutes.

These are drive estimates, not curb-to-room promises. Shared-shuttle waiting, hotel stops, traffic and large resort entrances add time. A shared ride to Cap Cana may be tolerable while the same model to Uvero Alto becomes the least pleasant hour of arrival.

The return changes too. Do not copy another traveler's pickup time because both bookings say Punta Cana. Our Punta Cana versus Cancun comparison puts these logistics beside the beach and resort trade-offs.

Leaving Punta Cana: The Quiet Detail People Miss

Confirm the departure eTicket and save its QR. PUJ recommends arriving at least three hours before US-bound flights because extra screening can apply. Include the drive from your actual resort area, not a generic Punta Cana estimate.

One surprising detail: PUJ operates without routine airline announcements. Watch the screens; a gate can change silently while you eat or shop.

Terminal A and B have separate check-in areas, so confirm the terminal with the airline that day. Arrange mobility assistance in advance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Dominican Republic eTicket free? +

Yes. The Dominican migration authority says the official eTicket costs nothing and normally takes about five minutes to complete. Use only eticket.migracion.gob.do. A website asking for a processing fee is a private intermediary, not the required government service.

When should I complete the eTicket for Punta Cana? +

Complete it after your flight number and accommodation details are settled, but before airline check-in. Save the generated QR code to your phone and keep the application code. You need eTicket coverage for both entry and departure, so confirm the exit portion before the trip home.

Can I take Uber from Punta Cana Airport? +

Uber currently provides PUJ pickup instructions, and the airport links to a rideshare pickup map. The meeting point may not be beside your nearest exit, so follow the live directions in the app and verify the driver, plate and car before boarding. Never accept an off-app ride from someone claiming to be your Uber driver.

How long is the transfer from PUJ to Punta Cana resorts? +

Dominican Republic Tourism lists about 15 minutes to Punta Cana and Cap Cana, 30 minutes to Bavaro, El Cortecito and Arena Gorda, and 45 minutes to Macao and Uvero Alto. Traffic, rain and the exact resort entrance can add time, so use those figures as planning ranges rather than promises.

Is a private transfer from Punta Cana Airport worth it? +

For a first visit, a late arrival, children or several bags, usually yes. You pay for a named meeting plan, a vehicle sized for the group and no curbside negotiation. An authorized taxi or app ride can work well for confident travelers, but compare the total vehicle quote rather than assuming a shared per-person fare is automatically cheaper.

Your PUJ Arrival Plan in One Minute

Five actions remove nearly every avoidable airport problem.

Complete the free official eTicket and save its QR code offline.
Save the operator name, meeting point and phone number, not just a booking logo.
After customs, speak only to the representative who can verify your booking.
For taxi or Uber, confirm the fare or app identity before boarding.
Before departure, confirm the eTicket, terminal and a pickup that reaches PUJ three hours before a US-bound flight.
Final verdict

For most first-time visitors, the best PUJ transfer is the one arranged before the flight, not the cheapest voice at the exit.

Complete the free government eTicket, prebook a correctly sized private vehicle, and walk through arrivals with the operator's instructions already on your phone. Use an authorized taxi or an in-app Uber pickup when you understand the meeting process and prefer flexibility.

The airport is not the trap. Arriving without a plan is.
