Flights to Egypt: Charters, Regular Flights, and Airports

Egypt remains one of the most popular destinations for beach vacations, especially during the autumn-winter season. However, when planning a trip, many face the question: fly charter or choose a regular flight.

The type of flight affects not only the ticket price, but also baggage, date flexibility, refund options, and even the arrival airport. In this material, we break down how flights to Egypt work in 2025–2026, and what to pay attention to before buying a ticket.

1. What types of flights to Egypt exist

You can get to Egypt in two main ways: charter flights and regular airline flights. Both options are actively used by tourists but suit different types of trips.

Charter flights

Charters are flights launched by tour operators based on tourist demand. They primarily target resort destinations and are most often sold as part of tour packages, though sometimes available separately.

Regular flights

Regular flights are operated by airlines on a fixed schedule year-round. They most often fly to major aviation hubs like Cairo, as well as popular resorts — Hurghada and Sharm el-Sheikh.

2. Charter flights to Egypt

Charter flights are the most common way to reach Egyptian resorts during high tourist season.

How charters work

Charter programs are formed by tour operators during periods of increased demand — most often from October to April. Main destinations: Hurghada, Sharm el-Sheikh, and Marsa Alam.

Advantages of charter flights

  • Cost often 40–60% lower than regular flights
  • Direct flights straight to resort airports
  • Baggage usually included in the price

Disadvantages and limitations

  • Limited refund and date change options
  • Departure time may be confirmed closer to the trip
  • No bonus miles or loyalty programs

Also, there's a rule: arrival and departure type must match. If you arrived by charter, you must depart by charter flight.

3. Regular flights to Egypt

Regular flights suit those who prefer flexibility, stable schedules, and independent route planning.

How regular flights operate

Main international flights go to Cairo, as well as Hurghada and Sharm el-Sheikh. Connections through major hubs are often used.

Advantages of regular flights

  • Fixed schedule
  • Flexible fares and refunds
  • Miles and bonus accrual

4. Main Egyptian airports for tourists

Sharm el-Sheikh (SSH)

Red Sea resorts, diving, direct charters and regular flights.

Hurghada (HRG)

One of Egypt's busiest tourist airports.

Cairo (CAI)

The country's main aviation hub and convenient connection point.

Marsa Alam (RMF)

Peaceful resort, charters, and nature without mass tourism.

5. Important flight nuances in 2025–2026

  • Arrival and departure type must match
  • Baggage allowances depend on the airline
  • Visa is issued on arrival
  • Transition to digital entry cards

Quick checklist before buying a ticket

  • Decide: charter or regular flight
  • Check baggage and refund policy
  • Clarify arrival airport
  • Verify dates and return flight type

Conclusion: Egypt in 2025–2026 remains an accessible destination. The right flight choice saves money, time, and avoids unnecessary stress.
