Frequently Asked Questions

Which Punta Cana area is best for a first visit? +

Bavaro is usually the simplest first base if you want a busy beach, many resorts and easy access to restaurants and excursions. Choose a quieter zone only when that resort-first rhythm is intentional.

Is Cap Cana better than Bavaro? +

Cap Cana is better for travelers prioritizing privacy, polished luxury and a quieter setting. Bavaro is better for a more social, active holiday. Neither is automatically better; they deliver different weeks.

Who should stay in Uvero Alto? +

Uvero Alto suits travelers who want a newer resort, more space and less crowding, and are comfortable treating the resort as the destination. It is a weak fit for people who expect to go out every evening.
