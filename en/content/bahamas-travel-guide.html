Bahamas Travel Guide: Nassau, Paradise Island & Out Islands (Exuma, Eleuthera)

Less than an hour in the air from South Florida puts you into an archipelago with two opposite personalities: big casino resorts and water parks around Nassau and Paradise Island, or quiet sandbars and pink-sand villages across the Out Islands.

Few places close to the U.S. East Coast create a wider gap between drone footage and first-time bookings than the Bahamas. Plenty of travelers reserve a long weekend in Nassau picturing empty, gin-clear sandbars, only to step out into a busy capital sharing its harbor with casino towers, water parks, and up to six docked cruise ships. Those quiet turquoise flats are real, but they sit 35 to 45 minutes further out by regional prop plane in the Out Islands—officially called the Family Islands by the Bahamas Ministry of Tourism.

The second surprise usually waits on the hotel folio. Unlike Cancun or Punta Cana, where all-inclusive resorts dominate, most major Bahamian properties price on a European Plan (Room-Only) basis. Once you layer the statutory 10% Bahamian Value Added Tax (VAT), property-level resort fees, and customary 15% dining gratuities onto a room rate, four nights in Nassau can match the cost of a full week in Mexico—a contrast we unpack in our Cancun vs Bahamas comparison.

Quick Answer: Where to Base Yourself in the Bahamas

For a first visit or a quick 3-to-4-night East Coast break, stay on Paradise Island or Cable Beach (West Nassau), where a 15-to-35-minute ride from the airport puts you straight onto resort pools, water slides, and walkable restaurants. When you have five or more nights and care far more about empty sapphire sandbars, pink sand, and boat days than casinos or nightlife, look past Nassau and fly to Great Exuma, Harbour Island, or Eleuthera.

  • Cable Beach / West Nassau: 15–20 minutes from Lynden Pindling Airport (NAS); home to Baha Mar and Sandals Royal Bahamian, calm reef-sheltered swimming, and no downtown cruise congestion.
  • Paradise Island: 30–35 minutes from NAS across two harbour bridges; anchored by Atlantis (141-acre Aquaventure water park), Four Seasons Ocean Club, and wide Atlantic-facing Cabbage Beach.
  • Great Exuma & Cays (GGT): Reached via nonstop flights from Miami, Atlanta, Charlotte, or a 35-to-45-minute hop from NAS; shallow electric-blue sandbars, easy skiff rentals, and the logical home base for the Exuma Cays and swimming pigs.
  • Harbour Island & Eleuthera (ELH): Three-mile Pink Sands Beach and golf-cart lanes on Harbour Island (via a 5-minute water taxi from North Eleuthera) paired with 110 miles of rugged, self-drive coves on mainland Eleuthera.

Geography & Base Choice: Nassau vs Paradise Island vs the Out Islands

On a map, the Bahamas stretches across 700 islands and 2,000 cays covering 500 miles of shallow Atlantic banks. On the ground, nearly 70% of the population and most hotel rooms sit on 21-by-7-mile New Providence (home to downtown Nassau, Cable Beach, and NAS airport) and bridge-linked Paradise Island. Fly past New Providence and Grand Bahama, and high-rise towers give way to single-runway airstrips and the Great Bahama Bank—a 5-to-15-foot shelf of white oolitic sand that gives the water its electric turquoise glow. To see how the archipelago stacks up against Turks and Caicos, Jamaica, or Aruba, check our Caribbean island comparison.

Even on New Providence, picking between Paradise Island and Cable Beach changes your week. Paradise Island is built around Atlantis Paradise Island—five hotel towers (from the mid-range Coral to the all-suite Cove and Reef) surrounding the 141-acre Aquaventure water park and marine habitats—alongside the quiet Four Seasons Ocean Club further east. Five miles west of downtown Nassau, Cable Beach centers on Baha Mar (Grand Hyatt, SLS, and Rosewood), which pairs the region's largest casino and the 15-acre Baha Bay beachfront water park with calmer north-shore water shielded from open-Atlantic swell.

Paradise Island (Atlantis & Ocean Club)

Aquaventure's river rapids, marine lagoons, and wide Atlantic sand along Cabbage Beach set the rhythm here. You can walk everywhere inside Marina Village, though crossing into Nassau takes a cab or harbour ferry.

Cable Beach & West Nassau (Baha Mar / Sandals)

Newer rooms, gentler reef-sheltered swimming, a 15-to-20-minute airport transfer, and clean separation from downtown cruise docks make this the easier New Providence base. Couples seeking an all-inclusive check into Sandals Royal Bahamian next door.

Great Exuma & The Exuma Cays (GGT)

Days revolve around water color and tide charts rather than resort lobbies. Sleep near George Town or Tropic of Cancer Beach, take a rental skiff across Elizabeth Harbour to Stocking Island, and explore the Exuma Cays with a local captain.

Harbour Island & Mainland Eleuthera (ELH)

A 5-minute boat ride separates two distinct worlds: 3.5-mile Harbour Island pairs Pink Sands Beach with historic clapboard cottages and golf carts, while 110-mile Eleuthera trades polish for Glass Window Bridge cliffs and empty coves.

Entry Rules, NAS Airport, Inter-Island Flights & Swimming Pigs Logistics

Under Bahamas Department of Immigration entry rules, every international traveler arriving by air—including U.S. and Canadian citizens and children of all ages—must present a valid passport book and a return or onward ticket (U.S. Passport Cards and birth-certificate rules apply only to closed-loop sea cruises from U.S. ports and cannot be used to fly home). Passport validity and visa-free stay limits depend on your nationality:

  • United States citizens: Per U.S. Department of State Bahamas travel guidance, U.S. passports must remain valid for the entire duration of the stay (with two blank pages required), and no tourist visa is required for stays up to one month (30 days)—with longer stays up to eight months requiring an extension through the Bahamas Department of Immigration.
  • Canadian citizens: Per Government of Canada Bahamas travel advice, Canadian passports must be valid for at least six months beyond your expected departure date from the Bahamas; tourist visas are not required for stays up to eight months.
  • United Kingdom citizens: Per UK FCDO Bahamas entry requirements, your passport should be valid for at least six months from your date of departure; British visitors are normally granted visa-free entry for up to 30 days, with extensions possible up to a maximum of eight months via the Department of Immigration.
  • European Union & other nationalities: Because passport-validity rules and bilateral visa-waiver periods depend on your specific country of citizenship (and airlines often enforce six-month validity rules when routing through the U.S. or UK), verify the exact requirements for your passport on the official Bahamas Department of Immigration visa schedule and with your airline before booking.

For all nationalities, the exact initial stay granted is determined by the immigration officer at the port of entry and recorded on your passport entry stamp; remaining in the country past the stamped date requires an in-person extension from the Department of Immigration. Bahamian passenger departure tax is already bundled into commercial airfares, and the former pandemic Health Visa is permanently discontinued.

Once you clear the hall at Lynden Pindling International Airport (NAS), Cable Beach sits 15–20 minutes east and Paradise Island takes 30–35 minutes. Uber and Lyft do not operate in the Bahamas; curbside dispatchers use the official NAS zone taxi schedule, where fixed November 2026 fares for two passengers run about $32–$40 to Cable Beach and $45–$55 to Paradise Island, plus $2 for the bridge toll, $4–$5 per extra rider, and $2 per extra bag (carry cash, as many cabs decline cards). To reach the Out Islands, either book a nonstop flight from Miami, Charlotte, or Atlanta into Exuma (GGT) or North Eleuthera (ELH), or walk three minutes from NAS Terminal A to Domestic Terminal B for a 35-to-45-minute hop on Bahamasair, Western Air, or Southern Air. Leave 90–120 minutes between separate tickets—see our Caribbean island hopping guide for regional transfer rules.

1. U.S. CBP Preclearance at NAS Terminal C

Nassau operates a full U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP) Preclearance facility. On your return flight to the United States, you clear U.S. passport control and customs in Nassau and step off at a domestic U.S. gate with no further arrival queues. Weekend lines between 11:30 AM and 3:30 PM build fast—arrive 3 hours before departure (2.5 hours with Global Entry).

2. Out-Island Turboprops vs Potter's Cay Fast Ferries (November 2026 Fares)

  • 35–45 min domestic flights (about $190–$260 round-trip): Fastest link to ELH, GHB, MHH, and GGT. Standard Bahamasair domestic baggage allowances include one 50 lb (23 kg) checked bag, whereas smaller regional turboprops cap bags at 40–44 lbs.
  • Bahamas Ferries from Potter's Cay: High-speed catamarans operated by Bahamas Ferries reach Spanish Wells and Harbour Island in 2.5–3 hours (about $145–$190 round-trip), though winter fronts often make open-ocean crossings rough.
  • Harbour Island final mile: Land at ELH, take a 10-minute shared cab ($5/person) to Three Island Dock, and ride a 5-minute water taxi ($5/person) straight to Harbour Island's Government Dock.

3. Exuma Cays & Big Major Cay ("Swimming Pigs"): Nassau Day Trip vs Exuma Base

The famous swimming pigs live on Big Major Cay (Staniel Cay) in the central Exuma Cays, 85 miles southeast of Nassau. As of November 2026, an 8-hour Nassau powerboat tour (about $600–$700/person) spends nearly three hours each way pounding across open Exuma Sound swells, while a 35-to-45-minute plane day trip from NAS into Staniel Cay (about $650–$750/person) transfers you smoothly onto a local skiff for Pig Beach, Compass Cay nurse sharks, Thunderball Grotto, and Bitter Guana Cay iguanas. When you stay on Great Exuma instead, a full-day local boat tour covers those same stops for about $260–$320/person (~$580 per couple).

Real Bahamas Budget: Why Room-Only Dominates & How Taxes Stack Up

The Bahamian dollar (BSD) is pegged 1:1 to the U.S. dollar by the Central Bank of The Bahamas, and U.S. cash is accepted everywhere (spend or swap Bahamian change before leaving NAS). Because nearly all food, fuel, and consumer goods are imported at 1:1 parity, resort menus mirror Miami Beach and Manhattan.

Why Room-Only (EP) dominates over All-Inclusive: Both Atlantis and Baha Mar operate strictly on a European Plan (Room-Only) basis. True all-inclusive resorts are limited to a short list—primarily Sandals Royal Bahamian and Breezes on Cable Beach, adults-only Warwick Paradise Island, Viva Fortuna on Grand Bahama, and Sandals Emerald Bay on Exuma.

Before locking in a room—see our regional Caribbean hotel booking checklist—separate the statutory government tax administered by the Bahamas Department of Inland Revenue (10% VAT) from property-level resort fees, promotional levies under the Bahama Out Islands Promotion Board and Nassau/Paradise Island boards, and customary hospitality service charges:

🏨
30%–40% mandatory hotel markups over base rates — Nassau and Paradise Island resorts combine the statutory 10% Bahamian VAT with property- and board-level charges published in official resort terms (Atlantis and Baha Mar): up to 21% in combined room taxes and promotional/service levies (inclusive of the 10% VAT), a mandatory $55–$95+ nightly resort fee (such as $77 to $82.50 per night, VAT included, depending on the Atlantis tower: $70 + $7 VAT at The Coral and The Royal; $75 + $7.50 VAT at The Cove and The Reef), and separate mandatory daily housekeeping gratuities (up to $14+ per person, per night in standard rooms and higher in suites).
🍽️
Customary 15% auto-gratuity + statutory 10% VAT on food and drinks — Per Bahamas Ministry of Tourism visitor tipping guidance and resort dining menus, restaurants and bars automatically add a 15% service charge (gratuity) alongside the statutory 10% VAT (+25% total), turning an $18 cocktail and $28 burger into a ~$57.50 tab. Check your itemized slip before writing an extra 20% tip.
🛺
Cash taxis, bridge tolls, and Out-Island wheels (Nov 2026 rates) — Round-trip cabs from Paradise Island to Arawak Cay Fish Fry run about $35–$50 with tolls. Harbour Island golf carts rent for $65–$90/day, while Great Exuma or Eleuthera compact rental cars cost $85–$115/day with fuel around $6.50–$7.50/gallon.
🎟️
The Atlantis Aquaventure day-pass trap — Non-guest Atlantis Aquaventure day passes cost about $195–$255/adult ($115–$150/child) in peak weeks and sell out on heavy cruise days. Families usually save by booking Atlantis directly or staying next door at Comfort Suites Paradise Island (where full Aquaventure access is bundled).

Cruise Ship Crowds in Nassau & How to Time Your Beach Days

After its $300 million redevelopment, Nassau Cruise Port at Prince George Wharf can berth up to six ships simultaneously, sending 20,000 to 30,000 day passengers onto Bay Street, the Queen's Staircase, and Junkanoo Beach between 9:00 AM and 3:30 PM. Keep mobile data active across the islands (see our Caribbean regional eSIM guide), check daily berth counts on the port schedule, and work against the cruise clock—by 4:30 PM, downtown Nassau and Arawak Cay quiet down completely.

Dodging the 25,000-passenger midday surge: On four-to-six-ship days, skip Bay Street and the public eastern entrance to Cabbage Beach on Paradise Island between 9:30 AM and 3:30 PM. Spend midday on private resort sand at Cable Beach or Cove Beach, head 10 minutes west of Cable Beach to reef-protected Love Beach (Nirvana Beach Bar) or Clifton Heritage National Park, or save Arawak Cay Fish Fry for dinner after 5:30 PM.

The U.S. Department of State classifies The Bahamas at Level 2 (Exercise Increased Caution). Crime occurs mainly on New Providence and Grand Bahama in non-tourist inland neighborhoods south of downtown Nassau ("Over-the-Hill"), particularly after dark. The advisory also warns against renting independent jet skis from unlicensed beach hawkers on New Providence and Paradise Island due to safety and assault incidents (U.S. embassy personnel are prohibited from using them). Stick to licensed zone taxis and hotel-vetted excursion boats; Out Islands like Exuma, Harbour Island, and Eleuthera move at a quiet village pace.

Seasonality & Water Temps: Cooler Winter Swims vs Hurricane Window

Because the archipelago sits in the Atlantic north of the Caribbean Sea (21°N to 27°N, near Miami's latitude), seasonal climate data from the Bahamas Ministry of Tourism shows winter cold fronts between late December and February dropping daytime air to about 74–78°F (23–26°C), evenings into the mid-60s°F (18–19°C), and ocean water to a brisk 72–75°F (22–24°C). Shallow Exuma sandbars warm faster on sunny afternoons, but January visitors expecting 81°F southern Caribbean water will want heated resort pools and a light sweater after sunset.

  • Late March through June (The Sweet Spot): Air warms to 82–86°F (28–30°C), ocean temperatures reach 78–82°F (26–28°C), winter swells calm down for Exuma and Rose Island crossings, and room rates drop roughly 15–30% after Easter.
  • August through October (Peak Hurricane Risk & Closures): Within the June 1–November 30 season tracked by the NOAA National Hurricane Center, storm risk peaks from mid-August through October—when many Harbour Island and Eleuthera inns and restaurants close for annual maintenance. Book refundable rates and travel insurance for late-summer dates.

Which Bahamas Island Fits Your Trip?

Match your flight window, group style, and dining budget to one of these four setups:

Families & Short Hops (3–4 Nights)

Maximum pools, slides, and zero connecting flights

Land at NAS and split the call by water park: Atlantis (Paradise Island) wins for older kids who want the 141-acre Aquaventure complex and marine habitats, while Baha Mar (Cable Beach) gives you newer rooms, calmer bay water, and Baha Bay just 15 minutes from the airport.

Turquoise Water Purists (5–7 Nights)

Sandbars, nurse sharks, and swimming pigs without the marathon

Book a direct flight into Great Exuma (GGT). With a rental car for Tropic of Cancer Beach, a water taxi across Elizabeth Harbour to Stocking Island, and a $260–$320 local boat run up the Exuma Cays, you skip the exhausting 6-hour Nassau powerboat commute.

Boutique Couples & Anniversaries

Walkable pink sand, pastel streets, and golf-cart evenings

Connect through North Eleuthera (ELH) and take the 5-minute water boat to Harbour Island between March and June. Pick up a golf cart at the dock for three-mile Pink Sands Beach, or pair four nights there with a rental car on mainland Eleuthera.

Budget-Certainty Seekers

Capping Bahamas food and cocktail bills up front

Avoid Room-Only mega-resorts and lock in an adults-only all-inclusive: Sandals Royal Bahamian on Cable Beach (complete with its offshore island, Barefoot Cay) for upscale beach days, or Warwick Paradise Island on Nassau Harbour for a gentler nightly total.

Four Bahamas Planning Mistakes Worth Avoiding

Most budget blowouts and itinerary regrets here come down to four preventable missteps:

Mistake 01

Tipping 20% on top of the automatic 15% service charge. Almost every Bahamian restaurant and bar includes 15% gratuity plus 10% VAT on the printed check. Adding a habitual U.S. 20% tip pushes your tax-and-tip surcharge to 45% above menu prices.

Mistake 02

Sleeping downtown just to buy Atlantis day passes. Once you pay $195–$255 per adult for Aquaventure admission (assuming passes haven't sold out to cruise ships) plus bridge taxis, a family of four often spends more than booking Atlantis or neighboring Comfort Suites Paradise Island (where full water-park access is included).

Mistake 03

Enduring a 6-hour round-trip Nassau speedboat to Pig Beach in winter swells. Crossing 85 miles of open Exuma Sound each way batters passengers on windy days. Either take the 35-to-45-minute plane tour into Staniel Cay, or stay on Great Exuma.

Mistake 04

Arriving on Harbour Island in September expecting a full dining scene. While Nassau runs year-round, many Harbour Island and Eleuthera boutique hotels and restaurants shut down for annual maintenance throughout September and early October.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it better to stay in Nassau (Cable Beach), Paradise Island, or the Out Islands like Exuma? +

For a 3-to-5-night first trip, Cable Beach (Baha Mar, Sandals) or Paradise Island (Atlantis, Ocean Club) is the easiest call: both sit 15 to 35 minutes from Nassau Airport (NAS) with water parks, casinos, and dozens of restaurants. If your priority is empty neon-blue sandbars, pink sand, and zero cruise ships, fly 35 to 45 minutes beyond Nassau to Great Exuma (GGT) or North Eleuthera (ELH) for Harbour Island.

Why are the Bahamas more expensive than Mexico or Punta Cana, and is All-Inclusive better than Room-Only? +

The Bahamas pegs its dollar 1:1 to USD, imports most food and energy, and runs mainly on Room-Only (European Plan) pricing. Major resorts add up to 21% in combined room taxes and promotional levies (including the statutory 10% VAT), nightly resort fees ($55-$95+, such as $77 to $82.50 per night, VAT included, depending on the Atlantis tower), and mandatory daily housekeeping gratuities (up to $14+ per person/night in standard rooms)—typically adding 30-40% to base room rates—while dining bills automatically include a customary 15% service charge plus 10% VAT. Adults-only all-inclusives like Sandals Royal Bahamian or Warwick Paradise Island cap food costs up front, whereas Atlantis and Baha Mar remain strictly room-only.

Is the Exuma swimming pigs day trip from Nassau worth the price? +

A powerboat day tour from Nassau to Big Major Cay (Pig Beach) costs about $600-$700 per person (November 2026 rates) and spends nearly three hours each way pounding across open Exuma Sound swells. Flying 35-45 minutes from NAS into Staniel Cay (about $650-$750 per person) is far smoother for a day trip, while travelers focused on the Exuma Cays, Thunderball Grotto, and Compass Cay nurse sharks get much better value basing on Great Exuma, where local full-day boat tours run about $260-$320 per person.

How do you avoid cruise ship crowds in Nassau? +

Nassau Cruise Port at Prince George Wharf can berth up to six ships at once, bringing 20,000 to 30,000 passengers ashore between 9:00 AM and 3:30 PM. On four-to-six-ship days, skip downtown Bay Street, Junkanoo Beach, and the public eastern entrance to Cabbage Beach at midday. Instead, use private resort sand on Cable Beach or western Paradise Island, head west to Love Beach or Clifton Heritage Park, or take a morning boat to Rose Island.

Is the water in the Bahamas warm enough for swimming in January and February? +

Because the Bahamas sits in the Atlantic north of the Caribbean Sea (24°N to 26°N around Nassau and Eleuthera), winter cold fronts off Florida drop January and February daytime air to about 74-78°F (23-26°C) and ocean water to 72-75°F (22-24°C). That is swimmable on sunny afternoons across shallow Exuma sandbars or heated pools, but cooler than Aruba or Jamaica. For 80°F+ water before peak hurricane risk, visit late March through June.

Final verdict

Decide between resort entertainment and untouched sandbars before booking your flight, because Nassau and the Out Islands deliver opposite vacations.

For a quick 3-to-4-night hop from the U.S. East Coast, base at Cable Beach (Baha Mar or Sandals) or Paradise Island (Atlantis or Ocean Club)—and budget roughly 30%–40% (~38% on average) above base room rates plus 25% on food tabs so checkout holds no surprises. When the neon-blue flats and pink-sand beaches in the photos are the whole point of flying south, skip Nassau's mega-resorts and head straight to Great Exuma (GGT) or North Eleuthera (ELH) for Harbour Island.
